**Working title:** When Scene Residualization Fails: A Normal-Only Video Anomaly Detection Study  
**Venue style:** ICLR-style initial draft

## One-sentence contribution

We show that frozen DINOv2 anomaly scores undergo catastrophic Ped2/Avenue threshold shift,
while a preregistered low-capacity scene-residual mechanism neither removes scene identity
nor beats strong raw-feature scorers.

## Structure

### Abstract

- State the negative result immediately.
- Define strict source-threshold transfer versus final-test oracle curve metrics.
- Report raw Gaussian 0.6885 versus full SRN 0.6677 and cross-dataset FPR 1.0.
- End with the implication: rank metrics can conceal deployment failure, and scene
  residualization requires mechanism verification before scale-up.

### §1 Introduction — 1.25 pages

- Motivate normal-only transfer without a generic deep-learning opening.

### §3 Problem Formulation and Tested Mechanism — 1.5 pages

- Define normal-only source data, target test data, strict and calibrated tracks.
- Define $z\to c\to\hat u$, $r=P(z-\hat u)$, $q=a(c)$,

### §4 Experimental Protocol — 1.25 pages

- Official Ped2/Avenue assets, official DINOv2 ViT-S/14, every-frame preprocessing.
- Whole-video normal-only train/source-validation/target-calibration/test splits.
- Baselines and ablations, seeds, CPU runtime, thresholds, and uncertainty.
- Distinguish oracle test-curve TPR@FPR from source-fixed operating metrics.
- State absence of background features, ShanghaiTech source blocker, and claim ceiling.

### §5 Results — 2 pages

- Within-dataset sanity table.
- Joint seen-domain method table and Fig. 2.
- Cross-dataset threshold table and Fig. 3.
- Report matched-prototype deltas and strongest raw Gaussian comparison.
- Avoid ranking SRN as successful based on a 0.0024 matched-head delta.

### §6 Diagnostics and Limitations — 1.25 pages

- Scene probe and residual variance results.
- Source-normal checkpoint selection and bounded LR diagnostic.
- ELOS control is performance-equivalent to raw because raw has no learned checkpoint.
- Dataset, event-label, calibration, temporal smoothing, and statistical limitations.
- Current scientific verdict: STOP the mechanism claim; broader nuisance premise remains.

### §7 Conclusion — 0.5 pages

- Restate the empirical finding and negative mechanism conclusion.
- Give exact condition for revisiting: authoritative multi-scene data plus scene-probe
  reduction and non-degraded low-FPR/event retention.

annotations show the falsification outcome: scene identity remains perfectly decodable from

unseen-scene ELOS.
