## Round 2 direct coverage result

No verified paper satisfies M2+M3+M4+M5+M6 in VAD. The nearest verified threats split into three families:

- background/skeleton/object abstraction that removes scene appearance without learned residualization；
- meta/few-shot scene adaptation that covers ELOS-like adaptation but uses target frames；
- non-VAD residual / invariant AD that covers residual or invariance concepts but not VAD scene-predictable subtraction with controlled context.
