# Prior-Method Defect → Idea Traceability Matrix

**方向**：Normal-Only Video Anomaly Detection on General/Public VAD Benchmarks  
**日期**：2026-07-06  
**用途**：把 11 个候选 idea 逐一追溯到具体 prior method / paper 的具体缺陷，并规定必须用什么实验才能证明“idea 的设计确实修复了该缺陷”。

## 判定规则

- “针对缺陷”不等于“已经解决”；没有对应消融证据前只能视为假设。
- novelty risk 同时检查机制和实验 setting；把 domain generalization、calibration 或 distillation 套到 VAD 上本身不自动构成创新。
- 主贡献必须对应一个机制级缺陷；纯评测、后处理和训练策略默认只能作为 supporting contribution，除非产生新的、可复现的科学发现。

## Top-3 detailed traceability

### 1. SRN: what exactly it tries to repair

SRN combines three specific defect chains rather than responding to a generic “poor generalization” claim:

1. **Frozen-feature cross-dataset collapse**：Rashidi 2026 reports that CLIP, DINOv2, ResNet-50 and EfficientNet-B0 coupled with kNN remain strong mainly on the source/target diagonal, while off-diagonal performance averages near chance; replacing kNN with a PaDiM-style Mahalanobis head does not remove the failure. This points to representation/domain leakage rather than a single detector bug. SRN therefore intervenes before the normality head by removing the scene-predictable feature component.
2. **Scene-aware learning does not imply unseen-scene transfer**：HSC makes normal clusters more scene/object aware and handles diverse known-scene normality, but its scene-level contrast can reward separation by known scene identity. SRN asks the opposite causal question: which part of `z` is predictable from the camera/scene, and can normality be learned on the remaining event component?
3. **Context cannot simply be discarded**：NWPU explicitly includes scene-dependent anomalies, and EVAL uses location-dependent normal models. A completely invariant representation can turn “wrong place” behavior into normal behavior. Therefore SRN must include a controlled context path and report location-dependent versus generic anomalies separately.

The decisive evidence is not merely a higher average AUC. SRN must simultaneously reduce a scene-ID probe, improve off-diagonal/worst-target metrics, retain performance on scene-dependent anomalies, and beat trivial mean subtraction/domain-adversarial projections.

### 2. AMCN: why conditional relation rather than absolute feature normality

AMCN targets three score-confounding mechanisms:

1. **Direct appearance normality** treats background/viewpoint changes as novelty; the cross-dataset audit shows stronger semantic features do not automatically solve this.
2. **Direct motion normality** changes with perspective, frame rate, optical-flow scale and crowd density, so the same physical event can occupy different embedding regions across cameras.
3. **Reconstruction/prediction error** mixes anomaly evidence with blur, texture and domain shift, while expressive predictors can also reconstruct/predict abnormal inputs.

AMCN's proposed fix is narrow: use normal clips to learn which motion embeddings are compatible with a given semantic context, then score the conditional residual. It is only defensible if it outperforms appearance-only, motion-only, concatenation and unconditional prediction under camera/frame-rate shifts. Otherwise it is simply another two-stream prediction baseline.

### 3. ELOS: why it belongs inside SRN

Most normal-only objectives optimize reconstruction, prototype compactness or semantic contrast over the scenes already present in a batch. They do not make a held-out camera an optimization target. Multi-scene data alone therefore permits shortcuts through scene identity.

ELOS changes the training unit: each episode withholds a normal scene and tests whether the shared residual normality mechanism remains calibrated on it. This directly simulates unseen-scene deployment. However, episodic domain generalization is not sufficiently novel by itself. ELOS should be framed as the training principle that makes the SRN mechanism testable; the key ablation is identical SRN architecture with random batching versus scene-balanced batching versus true held-out-scene episodes.

## Decision summary

- **Primary path**：SRN mechanism + ELOS training principle.
- **Mechanistically distinct backup**：AMCN.
- **Conditional support**：scene-aware mixture, prototype transport, source-only rank calibration, temporal false-alarm suppression.
- **Baselines/protocols**：object-relation graph, counterfactual retrieval, stress-test suite, masked distillation.
- 未完成独立评审和上述 decisive ablations 前，任何 novelty 判定都只是风险分级，不是确认。
